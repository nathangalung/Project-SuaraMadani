# Sadar Bersuara Design Guide

This file is the design contract for `app/components/` and `app/styles.css`. The look is the `suarakm-web` mockup (git `40ac3d9`), ported in full at the user's request: its components, sizes, colours, fonts, shapes and motion, placed where the PLAN flow needs them. Colours and fonts come from the tenant theme (PLAN §9). The km.itb.ac.id look is the **KM ITB tenant theme**; the platform default stays neutral and carries no KM ITB file.

Order of authority: the user's product decisions and the PLAN flow, then the mockup look, then outside patterns. Where a component needs an outside pattern, the sources are large private product companies and research firms (GitHub Primer, Shopify Polaris, Apple HIG, Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Airbnb, Baymard, NN/g), listed under Sources at the end. No government website or government design system is a reference. There is no masthead (removed 19 Oct 2026, team feedback, PLAN §6.1).

Rules here are enforced in review. Contrast is enforced in CI by `app/styles.test.ts` (defaults) and `db/seed/theme.test.ts` (KM ITB seed).

The seven background utilities are Tailwind `@utility` rules, so variants such as `lg:bg-hero` work. Tailwind emits one only when a source file names it as a literal class, never as `bg-${name}`.

How a tenant overrides them: the defaults live in `@layer base` on `:root`. `app/root.tsx` writes the tenant's `organizations.theme` values as an unlayered `<style>` block, which beats every layer. A tenant only writes the keys it changes. `server/theme.server.ts` accepts a 6-digit hex for colour keys. Background keys (`*Bg`) take up to 5 layers: `linear-gradient(<n>deg, …)` with 2 to 4 hex or `transparent` stops, `radial-gradient(<w>% <h>% at <x>% <y>%, #hex <n>%, transparent <n>%)`, and a plain hex only as the last layer. Anything else (`url()`, other functions, keywords) is dropped.

Pairs that must pass. The list lives in `shared/theme-contrast.ts`; `app/styles.test.ts` checks it against the defaults and `db/seed/theme.test.ts` against the KM ITB seed merged over the defaults. The theme form at `/kelola/pengaturan` checks the same list before saving (below).

- Text, 4.5:1 or better: ink, ink-muted, ink-soft, accent, danger and success on surface and surface-raised (success on surface only); accent on accent-soft and brand-soft; on-accent on accent, accent-hover, brand, brand-hover, pop-strong, danger and success; danger-ink on danger-soft; success-ink on success-soft; surface and surface-raised on ink; surface-raised on ink-hover; highlight on ink; ink on highlight, line-subtle, chip, accent-soft, brand-soft, mint-soft and success-soft.
- Large text only, 3:1 or better: on-accent on pop (hero title box), brand on surface (the big "404").
- Components, 3:1 or better: line and focus on surface and surface-raised; highlight and mint on ink; focus on every stop of the header, hero (above the mask fade), page, home-list, footer and detail backgrounds; highlight on every band stop. Focus on ink is 2.18:1, so `app/styles.css` switches the ring to highlight inside `bg-ink` and `bg-band`.
- Every colour stop of the background tokens: ink on header, hero, page, home-list, footer and detail stops at 4.5:1; accent (the draft link, footer links and page links such as "Di Halaman Ini") on header, footer, home-list and page stops at 4.5:1; ink-muted and ink-soft on page and detail stops at 4.5:1; on-accent on every band stop at 4.5:1.
- The hero ring check skips the last stop, because the hero mask fades it out (`ringStops`). Focusable hero content sits above that fade.
- Placement rule, not a test: inputs, checkboxes, radios and selects sit only on `surface` or `surface-raised`, never directly on a background token, and so do their labels, hints, field errors and required asterisks. On KM ITB, `line` is below 3:1 on every gradient stop (2.77 on page, 2.33 on detail) and `danger` is below 4.5:1 on page (4.41) and detail (3.72). A control or error that must sit on a gradient needs a `line` or `danger` pair on every stop first.

The mockup failures this fixes: `tinta-400` 2.43:1 on cream, `tinta-500` and `km-blue` `#176bff` 4.32:1 on cream, input border `navy/15`. The mockup's alias palettes (`krem-*`, `tinta-*`, `laut-*`, `biru-*` …) are gone.

### Theme Form (`/kelola/pengaturan`)

Any admin edits all 38 tokens in five groups (`features/campus/theme-editor.tsx`): colour hex fields with a swatch and the default as placeholder, background fields in the gradient grammar above, and three font selects over the four known stacks. Empty or default values are not stored. The server checks the `server/theme.server.ts` grammar (`isThemeValue`), then `checkTheme` (`shared/theme-contrast.ts`), which runs every CI pair list (text 4.5, large 3, UI 3, every background stop, `ringStops`) over `shared/theme-defaults.ts` (`THEME_DEFAULTS`, kept equal to `app/styles.css` by a test); any failure blocks the save and lists "{fg} di atas {bg} ({stop}): ratio:1, minimal min:1". `brandAssets` is kept and not editable. A live preview applies the typed values as scoped `--theme-*` variables (style attribute, client only). Re-running `bun db/seed/organizations.ts` in dev overwrites a theme saved there.

## Fonts

- `app/fonts/` holds latin-subset woff2 files from Fontsource 5.3.0, with each family's OFL-1.1 licence beside them: Tilt Warp 400, Aleo 400 and 700, Barlow 400 and 600 (at most 2 weights per family, PLAN §9). Total 87,880 bytes. `@font-face` rules in `app/styles.css` use `font-display: swap` and relative URLs, so Vite emits them as hashed `/assets/*` files served for 1 year, immutable (architecture.md cache table).
- Each themed family has a metric-matched fallback face (`"Aleo Fallback"` on Georgia, `"Tilt Warp Fallback"` and `"Barlow Fallback"` on Arial or Liberation Sans) with `size-adjust` and ascent, descent and line-gap overrides, so text keeps its line breaks when the webfont swaps in. `server/theme.server.ts` inserts the fallback family after the webfont when it writes a tenant's font stack. Values: hhea metrics of each woff2 against the Windows Georgia and Arial files, average advance weighted by the character frequency of `messages/id.json`. Recompute them when a font file changes.
- `app/root.tsx` preloads the 400 face of the tenant's heading font (`crossorigin="anonymous"`), so the H1 font starts downloading with the HTML instead of after the CSS.
- A browser downloads a face only when a theme uses it, so tenants on the default theme load no font files.
- The platform default theme uses one family: the system stack.
- KM ITB usage: Aleo for body, menus, card titles, numbers (bold) and form hints (since 21 Oct 2026); Barlow for form labels and legends, choice labels, the report step list titles, badges and chart text; the report step question H1 (`STEP_HEADING`) is Tilt Warp since 21 Oct 2026, the `/tentang` pattern of a Tilt Warp heading, Barlow titles and Aleo lines (team request); Tilt Warp only for H1, section headings, page bands and the primary button. Never Tilt Warp for numbers, logos, card titles or small text.
- Barlow has no 700: `font-bold` on `font-ui` text renders 600.

## Logo

The footer logo is `loading="lazy"` and carries the same `srcSet`, so a 3× phone reuses the 168px file the header already fetched.

SVG is refused (script risk). The uploaded logo replaces the header and footer image (`shellData.logoSrc`); `brandAssets` ornaments stay.

### Flow Rules

- **One purpose.** The H1 and the first paragraph say what the page is for. A page a first-time visitor can reach from the header says why it matters to them (`/masuk`: to see the status of your reports, and "Daftar" for a first account).
- **Reading order.** What this is, why it matters, what you can do, what happens next. Home: hero (what, do) → "Tuntutan yang Dikawal" when any tuntutan is published (the main content after the hero, team decision 19 October 2026, PLAN §8.2) → "Isu Terbaru" (proof). Home has no Cara Kerja section (removed 19 Oct 2026 at the team's request); the steps live on `/tentang`. `/data`: header, notes, Angka Utama, claim and moderation cards, Tanggapan Instansi, Tuntutan Publik, Peta Laporan dan Tuntutan, charts, ranking, Tentang Data Ini, "Jelajah". Issue page: PLAN §6.1 order. Success page: confirmation → the report label → what happens next → one button.
- **One next step at the end.** A page ends with one primary action: home "Lihat semua isu" under "Isu Terbaru", or one "Jelajah" whenever that list is empty, so the page never ends on an empty state, `/isu` and issue pages "Buat laporan", `/data` "Jelajah", `/tentang`, `/privasi`, `/pedoman` "Buat laporan", `/tuntutan` "Mengalami masalah publik di daerah Anda? Buat laporan" after the register (only when the list has results; with nothing published the empty state itself ends with "Buat laporan"), `/tuntutan/:code` its aside like `/isu/:code` (the follow card "Ikuti tuntutan ini"), success and signed-in `/masuk` "Laporan saya" (signed-in `/masuk` also offers "Keluar dan ganti akun" as a secondary button, a different destination: it signs out and returns to `/masuk`), `/lacak` "Masuk" signed out and "Buat laporan" signed in. `/kelola` home ends with "Buka log audit"; the close-account done state with "Buka antrean laporan". On the apex, `/tentang`, `/privasi` and `/pedoman` end with "Pilih kampus" (`/#kampus`) instead of "Buat laporan" (`ReportButton`, `useNational`), and the apex home ends with "Tentang Sadar Bersuara". Error pages are the one exception: "Ke beranda" plus one recovery link (404 "Jelajah", or "Pilih kampus" on the apex; 403 "Masuk" or "Verifikasi dua langkah"; the 410 of a removed issue "Jelajah" plus "Pedoman konten"). A short footnote (the lock privacy note on `/lacak` and `/masuk`, the draft note under the report card) may follow the last action; it is never a second action.
- **Say a thing once.** No sentence or instruction repeats inside a page. On `/tuntutan/:code` the Riwayat timeline names a subtuntutan event by its title and status line only ("Status subtuntutan 3 berubah", then "Menjadi Direspons · Dipenuhi", the state after the change, so the verb appears once); the note, source and link live once, under that subtuntutan's "Pembaruan" (`demandHistory` drops the body when the event has an item), and that title links to the subtuntutan row (`#sub-<n>`); the row's "Pembaruan" shows the status each update recorded. Publish, close and reopen entries keep their note. Copy that depends on the visitor's state follows it (the success page tells a signed-in reporter where the status is, and a signed-out visitor to sign in first).
- **One control per destination in a view.** Two controls that open the same place in the same view are a bug (the draft link and the "Lapor" menu item, a back link beside a sub-nav link). Site chrome does not count.
- **One owner per job.** Each job has one page: status of my reports `/lacak`, issue detail `/isu/:code`, numbers `/data`, the rules `/pedoman`, data handling `/privasi`, who and why `/tentang`. Another page may summarise it in a few lines (the `/tentang` method cards summarise `/pedoman#pemeriksaan`); the owner never links back to the summary.
- **No dead ends and no empty promises.** Empty states say when content appears and offer the page's next step once. No link or sentence promises a feature that is not built (Trust Copy).

## Colour Rules

- Base: surface background, raised cards, ink text.
- One accent: buttons, links, the active state.
- Colour carries meaning only for success (green) and danger (red). Red is for errors and emergencies only: one tone per status across the app, the critical tone only for states that need action (Polaris Badge; Stripe badge action levels), and never colour alone (Apple HIG Accessibility).
- Categories: neutral chip plus a small square in the category colour (same colour as in charts).
- Tuntutan marks follow the same rule: no red for waiting or refused states. "Belum direspons" and "Belum dipenuhi" use `CircleDashed` in `ink-soft`; "Sebagian dipenuhi" `Contrast` in `accent`; "Dipenuhi" and "Direspons" `Check` in `success-ink`; "Ditolak" `Ban` in `ink`. In `ProgressSquares` a refused subtuntutan is a hollow square (2px `ink-soft` border, no fill), so it never reads as one more filled, done square (review round 3). The mockup's red X is not ported.

- Boxes only for objects: something you click (issue card), fill in (form panel), or a dashboard panel.

## Motion

The mockup's motion is ported in full (user request), CSS first, with no animation library. Values match the mockup: its curve `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` is practically Linear's `--ease-out-quint` (`cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1)`) and close to the ease-out Stripe uses most (`cubic-bezier(.25, 1, .5, 1)`). The duration tokens that drop to `0s` under reduced motion follow Apple's iPhone page, which sets its enter and exit duration tokens to `0ms` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; Stripe and Linear also switch motion off there. Exits shorter than entries follow NN/g, and CSS before JS follows Vercel's interface guidelines (Sources).

Tokens in `app/styles.css`:

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ease-out` (Tailwind `ease-out`) | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The one signature curve: reveals, stagger, dialog and toast entries, hovers |
| `--ease-scroll` | `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)` | Scroll-linked hero only |
| `--ease-exit` | `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 1, 1)` | Exits |

| `--dur-backdrop` | 200ms | Dialog backdrop fade-in |
| `--dur-dialog-in` | 220ms | Dialog entry (opacity, scale .96, translate .5rem) |

Rules:

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets every `--dur-*` token to `0s`, and the base layer still cuts any other animation or transition to 0.01ms. `app/styles.test.ts` fails if a token is missing from the reduce block or an exit is not shorter than the entries.
- Content is visible by default. `.reveal` and `.stagger` hide content, so only JS adds them, and only to elements still offscreen at mount; no markup ships them (`app/styles.test.ts`).
- Entrances and exits move only transform and opacity. Hovers may fade colours. Nothing animates layout properties; reserve the space of anything that moves, so motion never shifts layout.
- Header logo (KM ITB set only, team request 21 Oct 2026): the plain hand (`logo-tangan-polos`) sits over the curled hand (`logo-tangan`) as `.logo-intro` and crossfades plain, curl, plain, curl (`logo-intro` keyframes, `--dur-logo` 1400 ms, 3 alternate passes, `ease-in-out`, holds of 25% at each end), then rests on the curl. 4.2 s in all, under the 5 s of WCAG 2.2.2, so no pause control is needed (`app/styles.test.ts` checks it). A mouse hover loops the same morph through the Web Animations API and finishes the current pass on leave; touch never triggers it. The animation is declared only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; otherwise `.logo-intro` is `opacity: 0` and only the curl shows. The footer logo stays still. Both frames come from one measured box, so the fingers do not move during the fade.
- Home hero (KM ITB tenant only): the mockup scroll choreography as CSS scroll-driven animation (`.hero-scroll`, unlayered in `app/styles.css`, 0 KB JS). The text column fades (opacity and translate only, so the H1 stays in the accessibility tree); only the `.hero-actions` wrapper (search card and report buttons) ends `visibility: hidden`, so invisible controls take no focus or click. While scrolled past the hero, Tab skips those controls; Home or PageUp brings them back. The graphic has no `will-change`, so Chrome rasterises it at the largest keyframe scale and the 1.45 bubble stays sharp. The bubble moves to the centre, grows to 1.45 and drops out while the light background fades to the band. The first section after the hero rises under the stage (`.hero-scroll` ends with `margin-bottom: -70vh`): "Tuntutan yang Dikawal" when any tuntutan is published, otherwise the `bg-home-list` part with "Isu Terbaru". Active only at `min-width: 64rem`, `min-height: 54rem`, `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`; the height gate keeps the whole text column, report buttons included, on screen while the stage is sticky. Everything else gets the static hero.
- Reveal and stagger where the code uses them today: Home (section headings, the "Tuntutan yang Dikawal" card rows, showcase lists), `/data` (section headings, the Angka Utama numbers, the "Tuntutan Publik" register and the Peringkat Isu rows; the two tuntutan registers use `useStagger` on the `<ol>` with `--i` per row), and the staff lists `/kelola/anggota` (member list) and `/admin` (campus list), which use `useStagger` only. The apex national home ships no JS, so it uses the CSS-only `.view-rise` and `.view-rise-list` and `.bar-grow`, declared only under `@media screen and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())` (`app/styles.test.ts` checks the guard), so content is visible in browsers without the feature, under reduced motion and in print. Other working pages (`/isu`, `/tuntutan`, `/tuntutan/:code`, `/lapor/*`, `/lacak`, `/masuk`, the rest of `/kelola` including the tuntutan pages) get hover, press, dialog and toast motion only. With emulated `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `/` and `/data` carry no `.stagger` or `.reveal` class (browser check). The stagger on the two staff lists departs from the earlier "Home only" rule and is recorded here as built; remove it there if the rule should stay strict.
- No motion on keyboard-repeated actions: focus rings, the mobile menu and copy-button state change instantly.
- Never: large-area parallax, autoplay video, animated gradients, `backdrop-filter` over scrolling content, shake on error, page transitions.

- Fonts: chart text and table cells `font-ui` (Barlow on KM ITB); numbers bold body font, as the mockup.

### Map Boundaries

`app/features/data/provinces.topo.json` holds the 38 provinces, keyed by Kemendagri code with cahyadsn/wilayah names (MIT). Geometry: OpenStreetMap `admin_level=4` relations (all 38 carry `ref:ID:kemendagri`), polygons from polygons.openstreetmap.fr, clipped to the OSM simplified land polygons (osmdata.openstreetmap.de, 13 Oct 2026) because the province relations include sea areas, islands under 100 km² dropped, simplified to 2% (mapshaper 0.7.70, run once from a scratch script that is not in the repo), quantised to 20,000. The topology is read only on the server. The projected paths in `provinces.svg` are public (the map loads them), so that file carries an ODbL notice and is released under ODbL as PLAN §5.9 requires for downloadable derived data; the page carries PLAN §5.9's attribution line. No BIG data is used. Licence and attribution terms: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/; land polygons: https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/data/land-polygons.html. Replace it with the PostGIS import from the Phase 0 spike when that lands.

## Email

Emails (`server/emails/layout.ts`, `render.ts`) carry the same voice as the site in a layout mail clients render: system fonts, ink `#08266e`, soft ink `#2c3a66`, surface `#fff8e5` behind a raised `#ffffff` 600 px column, line `#d5d9e4`, and the tenant `theme.brand` (default `#0f55d6`) for the top bar and the one square action button. Facts sit in a table with left `th` labels and vertically centred cells; long URLs hide behind link labels; every follower email ends with "Anda menerima email ini karena mengikuti isu {code}." (or its tuntutan twin, with the tuntutan code) and the per-issue or per-tuntutan unfollow link. A tuntutan mail carries the code, title, "{n} dari {total} subtuntutan dipenuhi" and the link. The sign-in code mail has the heading "Kode Verifikasi Sadar Bersuara" (`email_otp_heading`) and the subject "Kode verifikasi Sadar Bersuara"; the invitation mail is "Undangan menjadi admin {operator} di Sadar Bersuara" with the facts Organisasi and Peran (Admin). The WhatsApp code is one short plain-text line with no link (lower ban risk). Every email has an HTML and a text part.

## Forbidden ("Too AI")

- Rounded white cards with shadows, rows of identical boxes for content that is really a list.
- Pill buttons (`rounded-full` is for real circles only) and red to pink to purple gradients.
- Gradient text, blurred blobs in corners.
- Small uppercase tracked labels above headings (eyebrows), arrows at the end of button text.
- A long one-sentence title in a section heading: move the sentence into `description`.
- Dark 3D, glass spheres, black neon backgrounds.

Quick check: `rg -nP "rounded-(2xl|3xl|full)|shadow-(?!floating|toast)|uppercase|tracking-" app/components app/features app/routes` should only find real circles and the letter-spaced report and verification codes.
