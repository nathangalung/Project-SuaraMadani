# Suara Madani Design Guide

This file is the design contract for `app/components/` and `app/styles.css`. The look is the `suarakm-web` mockup (git `40ac3d9`), ported in full at the user's request: its components, sizes, colours, fonts, shapes and motion, placed where the PLAN flow needs them. Colours and fonts come from the tenant theme (PLAN §9). The km.itb.ac.id look is the **KM ITB tenant theme**; the platform default stays neutral and carries no KM ITB file.

Order of authority: the user's product decisions and the PLAN flow, then the mockup look, then outside patterns. Where a component needs an outside pattern, the sources are large private product companies and research firms (GitHub Primer, Shopify Polaris, Apple HIG, Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Airbnb, Baymard, NN/g), listed under Sources at the end. No government website or government design system is a reference; the masthead exists because PLAN §9 requires it, not to copy one.

Rules here are enforced in review. Contrast is enforced in CI by `app/styles.test.ts` (defaults) and `db/seed/theme.test.ts` (KM ITB seed).

The seven background utilities are Tailwind `@utility` rules, so variants such as `lg:bg-hero` work. Tailwind emits one only when a source file names it as a literal class, never as `bg-${name}`.

How a tenant overrides them: the defaults live in `@layer base` on `:root`. `app/root.tsx` writes the tenant's `organizations.theme` values as an unlayered `<style>` block, which beats every layer. A tenant only writes the keys it changes. `server/theme.server.ts` accepts a 6-digit hex for colour keys. Background keys (`*Bg`) take up to 5 layers: `linear-gradient(<n>deg, …)` with 2 to 4 hex or `transparent` stops, `radial-gradient(<w>% <h>% at <x>% <y>%, #hex <n>%, transparent <n>%)`, and a plain hex only as the last layer. Anything else (`url()`, other functions, keywords) is dropped.

Pairs that must pass. The list lives in `shared/theme-contrast.ts`; `app/styles.test.ts` checks it against the defaults and `db/seed/theme.test.ts` against the KM ITB seed merged over the defaults. The theme form at `/kelola/pengaturan` checks the same list before saving (below).

- Text, 4.5:1 or better: ink, ink-muted, ink-soft, accent, danger and success on surface and surface-raised (success on surface only); accent on accent-soft and brand-soft; on-accent on accent, accent-hover, brand, brand-hover, pop-strong, danger and success; danger-ink on danger-soft; success-ink on success-soft; surface and surface-raised on ink; surface-raised on ink-hover; highlight on ink; ink on highlight, line-subtle, chip, accent-soft, brand-soft, mint-soft and success-soft.
- Large text only, 3:1 or better: on-accent on pop (hero title box), brand on surface (the big "404").
- Components, 3:1 or better: line and focus on surface and surface-raised; highlight and mint on ink; focus on every stop of the header, hero (above the mask fade), page, home-list, footer and detail backgrounds; highlight on every band stop. Focus on ink is 2.18:1, so `app/styles.css` switches the ring to highlight inside `bg-ink` and `bg-band`.
- Every colour stop of the background tokens: ink on header, hero, page, home-list, footer and detail stops at 4.5:1; accent (the draft link, footer links and page links such as "Di Halaman Ini") on header, footer, home-list and page stops at 4.5:1; ink-muted and ink-soft on page and detail stops at 4.5:1; on-accent on every band stop at 4.5:1.
- The hero ring check skips the last stop, because the hero mask fades it out (`ringStops`). Focusable hero content sits above that fade.
- Placement rule, not a test: inputs, checkboxes, radios and selects sit only on `surface` or `surface-raised`, never directly on a background token, and so do their labels, hints, field errors and required asterisks. On KM ITB, `line` is below 3:1 on every gradient stop (2.77 on page, 2.33 on detail) and `danger` is below 4.5:1 on page (4.41) and detail (3.72). A control or error that must sit on a gradient needs a `line` or `danger` pair on every stop first.

The mockup failures this fixes: `tinta-400` 2.43:1 on cream, `tinta-500` and `km-blue` `#176bff` 4.32:1 on cream, input border `navy/15`. The mockup's alias palettes (`krem-*`, `tinta-*`, `laut-*`, `biru-*` …) are gone.

### Theme Form (`/kelola/pengaturan`)

Any admin edits all 38 tokens in five groups (`features/campus/theme-editor.tsx`): colour hex fields with a swatch and the default as placeholder, background fields in the gradient grammar above, and three font selects over the four known stacks. Empty or default values are not stored. The server checks the `server/theme.server.ts` grammar (`isThemeValue`), then `checkTheme` (`shared/theme-contrast.ts`), which runs every CI pair list (text 4.5, large 3, UI 3, every background stop, `ringStops`) over `shared/theme-defaults.ts` (`THEME_DEFAULTS`, kept equal to `app/styles.css` by a test); any failure blocks the save and lists "{fg} di atas {bg} ({stop}): ratio:1, minimal min:1". `brandAssets` is kept and not editable. A live preview applies the typed values as scoped `--theme-*` variables (style attribute, client only). Re-running `bun db/seed/organizations.ts` in dev overwrites a theme saved there.

## Fonts

- `app/fonts/` holds latin-subset woff2 files from Fontsource 5.3.0, with each family's OFL-1.1 licence beside them: Tilt Warp 400, Aleo 400 and 700, Barlow 400 and 600 (at most 2 weights per family, PLAN §9). Total 87,880 bytes. `@font-face` rules in `app/styles.css` use `font-display: swap` and relative URLs, so Vite emits them as hashed `/assets/*` files served for 1 year, immutable (architecture.md cache table).
- Each themed family has a metric-matched fallback face (`"Aleo Fallback"` on Georgia, `"Tilt Warp Fallback"` and `"Barlow Fallback"` on Arial or Liberation Sans) with `size-adjust` and ascent, descent and line-gap overrides, so text keeps its line breaks when the webfont swaps in. `server/theme.server.ts` inserts the fallback family after the webfont when it writes a tenant's font stack. Values: hhea metrics of each woff2 against the Windows Georgia and Arial files, average advance weighted by the character frequency of `messages/id.json`. Recompute them when a font file changes.
- `app/root.tsx` preloads the 400 face of the tenant's heading font (`crossorigin="anonymous"`), so the H1 font starts downloading with the HTML instead of after the CSS.
- A browser downloads a face only when a theme uses it, so tenants on the default theme load no font files.
- The platform default theme uses one family: the system stack.
- KM ITB usage: Aleo for body, menus, card titles and numbers (bold); Barlow for labels, hints, badges and chart text; Tilt Warp only for H1, section headings, page bands and the primary button. Never Tilt Warp for numbers, logos, card titles or small text.
- Barlow has no 700: `font-bold` on `font-ui` text renders 600.

## Logo

`public/logo.svg` (367 bytes) and `public/favicon.svg` are the Suara Madani mark: a megaphone on an accent square. Every tenant uses it, except where the org theme names a brand set.

- The header (`size-11`, 44px) and footer (`size-14`, 56px) show the logo as `<img alt="">` next to the visible site name and tenant name, so the link's accessible name is the visible text (fixes the mockup's `label-content-name-mismatch`).
- `brandImages(set)` in `layout/brand-assets.ts` picks the files: `/logo.svg` and no nav star by default; the KM ITB emblem `/km-itb/logo-km.webp` (with `srcSet` `logo-km.webp 2x, logo-km-168.webp 3x`) and the nav star for `brandAssets: "km-itb"`. The root loader (`server/shell.server.ts`) sends the set, so the 403 shell, the auth header and the home ornaments (`routes/home.tsx` reads it with `useRouteLoaderData("root")`) all follow one gate.
- The header logo is not lazy (above the fold on every page; React emits an image preload for it, with `imagesrcset` for the KM ITB set, so each device fetches one file). The footer logo is `loading="lazy"` and carries the same `srcSet`, so a 3× phone reuses the 168px file the header already fetched.
- The KM ITB emblem is a temporary 112×106 WebP plus a 168×159 one for 3× screens (see Temporary KM ITB Assets). PLAN §9 asks for an SVG logo; replace the WebP when KM ITB supplies the official vector file.
- The favicon stays the Suara Madani mark on every tenant.
- An admin can upload a logo at `/kelola/pengaturan`: PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 96 KB (fits the 128 KB form body), sniffed by magic bytes, re-encoded in `web` by sharp (`failOn: warning`, 4096×4096 pixel cap, `autoOrient`, fit inside 672×168, WebP q90, no metadata) and stored in `organizations.logo_bytes`; it is served at `/logo/<sha256-16>.webp` with `immutable`, `nosniff` and `CSP: sandbox; default-src 'none'`. SVG is refused (script risk). The uploaded logo replaces the header and footer image (`shellData.logoSrc`); `brandAssets` ornaments stay.

## Information Architecture and Page Flow

PLAN §6 sets the routes. The menu and the flow rules below are a product owner decision (2026-10-13), extended on 2026-10-16 (one admin role, Tuntutan as a tab of Jelajahi Isu, sign-up). Every page is checked against them; each page's closing action is listed under One next step at the end.

### Flow Rules

- **One purpose.** The H1 and the first paragraph say what the page is for. A page a first-time visitor can reach from the header says why it matters to them (`/masuk`: to see the status of your reports, and "Daftar" for a first account).
- **Reading order.** What this is, why it matters, what you can do, what happens next. Home: hero (what, do) → Cara Kerja (what happens) → "Tuntutan yang Dikawal" when any tuntutan is published → the two PLAN lists (proof). `/data`: header, notes, Angka Utama, claim and moderation cards, Tanggapan Instansi, Tuntutan Publik, Peta Laporan dan Tuntutan, charts, ranking, Tentang Data Ini, "Jelajahi Isu". Issue page: PLAN §6.1 order. Success page: confirmation → the report label → what happens next → one button.
- **One next step at the end.** A page ends with one primary action: home "Lihat semua isu yang selesai terkonfirmasi", or one "Jelajahi Isu" whenever that last list is empty, so the page never ends on an empty state, `/isu` and issue pages "Buat laporan", `/data` "Jelajahi Isu", `/tentang`, `/privasi`, `/pedoman` "Buat laporan", `/tuntutan` "Mengalami masalah publik di daerah Anda? Buat laporan" after the register (only when the list has results; with nothing published the empty state itself ends with "Buat laporan"), `/tuntutan/:code` its aside like `/isu/:code` (the follow card "Ikuti tuntutan ini", then the quiet "Laporkan konten" line), success and signed-in `/masuk` "Laporan saya" (signed-in `/masuk` also offers "Keluar dan ganti akun" as a secondary button, a different destination: it signs out and returns to `/masuk`), `/lacak` "Masuk" signed out and "Buat laporan" signed in. `/kelola` home ends with "Buka log audit"; the close-account done state with "Buka antrean laporan". On the apex, `/tentang`, `/privasi` and `/pedoman` end with "Pilih kampus" (`/#kampus`) instead of "Buat laporan" (`ReportButton`, `useNational`), and the apex home ends with "Tentang Suara Madani". - **Say a thing once.** No sentence or instruction repeats inside a page. Publish, close and reopen entries keep their note. Copy that depends on the visitor's state follows it (the success page tells a signed-in reporter where the status is, and a signed-out visitor to sign in first).
- **One control per destination in a view.** Two controls that open the same place in the same view are a bug (the draft link and the "Lapor" menu item, a back link beside a sub-nav link). Site chrome and the masthead do not count.
- **One owner per job.** Each job has one page: status of my reports `/lacak`, issue detail `/isu/:code`, numbers `/data`, the rules `/pedoman`, data handling `/privasi`, who and why `/tentang`. - **No dead ends and no empty promises.** Empty states say when content appears and offer the page's next step once. No link or sentence promises a feature that is not built (Trust Copy).

## Colour Rules

- Base: surface background, raised cards, ink text.
- One accent: buttons, links, the active state.
- Colour carries meaning only for success (green) and danger (red). Red is for errors and emergencies only: one tone per status across the app, the critical tone only for states that need action (Polaris Badge; Stripe badge action levels), and never colour alone (Apple HIG Accessibility).
- Categories: neutral chip plus a small square in the category colour (same colour as in charts).
- Tuntutan marks follow the same rule: no red for waiting or refused states. "Belum direspons" and "Belum dipenuhi" use `CircleDashed` in `ink-soft`; "Sebagian dipenuhi" `Contrast` in `accent`; "Dipenuhi" and "Direspons" `Check` in `success-ink`; "Ditolak" `Ban` in `ink`. In `ProgressSquares` a refused subtuntutan is a hollow square (2px `ink-soft` border, no fill), so it never reads as one more filled, done square (review round 3). The mockup's red X is not ported.

## Boxes

- Boxes only for objects: something you click (issue card), fill in (form panel), or a dashboard panel.
- Lists, steps, statistics and principles use thin rules, not boxes: a 2px ink rule above, 1px subtle rules between rows, or a ruled table. One accepted exception: `/tentang` keeps the mockup TentangPage `KartuMisi` cards for its four steps, four method notes and the two public / never-public lists (the port brief asked for that page's design); its principles stay on hairlines (`LineGrid`).
- No shadows on content. Card edge: `border border-line-subtle`. Card link hover: `hover:border-ink`. The only shadows are `shadow-floating` (dialog, the header account menu and the mobile menu panel) and `shadow-toast` (toast), because they float above the page, plus the mockup's own `/tentang` shadows in the KM ITB set (`shadow-about-panel` is the mockup `shadow-xl` on the pastel panel, `shadow-about-card` its `0 2px 16px` black 6% on the cards). The default theme keeps the bordered card there.
- Depth comes from surface contrast (surface, raised, ink band, brand band). Gradients appear only through the tenant background tokens (`header-bg`, `bg-hero`, `bg-band`, `bg-page`, `bg-home-list`, `bg-footer`, `bg-detail`), never as component styling.
- Radii are constants, not tenant tokens: cards and badges square; buttons and toast 10px (`rounded-[10px]`); inputs 6px (`rounded-md`); dialog and report form card 12px (`rounded-xl`); home list cards 8px (`rounded-lg`); status chips on coloured cards 4px (`rounded`); the `/masuk` "Masuk"/"Daftar" segmented control 12px outside (`rounded-[12px]` on `bg-surface`) and 9px for each segment (`rounded-[9px]`, the outer radius minus the 3px inset).

## Headings and Copy

PLAN §8.3 applies to every string in `messages/id.json`:

- Formal "Anda" everywhere.
- **Title Case** (EYD V) for page titles, headings, section titles and table headers: capitalise every word except function words not at the start (*di, ke, dari, dan, atau, yang, untuk, dengan, pada, dalam, oleh, bagi, tentang, serta, tetapi, terhadap, per, saat, sebelum, jika, sampai, sejak*, and *soal* where it means *tentang*). Examples of the newer words: "Privasi sejak Awal", "Jujur soal Kemampuan", the email fact "Berlaku sampai". Examples: "Isu di Daerah Anda", "Periksa dan Kirim", "Ada Masalah". This replaces the mockup rule "huruf kalimat biasa" for section headings.
- Sentence case for labels, buttons, statuses and table cells: "Buat laporan", "Sedang diperiksa". Staff form labels and panel titles are sentence case even where the public heading is Title Case: "Teks tuntutan" (form label; the public H2 is "Teks Tuntutan"), "Policy brief" (editor panel; the public H4 is "Policy Brief"), the button "Buat tuntutan" (the page H1 is "Buat Tuntutan"). - Error messages say how to fix the problem and never use "tidak valid".
- Examples go in hints under the label, never in placeholders.
- Numbers and dates go through `Intl` with `id-ID`.
- No emoji, no arrows at the end of button text.
- Evidence copy says "berkas" for files and "bukti" for evidence (never only "foto"); the step 5 title is "Ada Foto atau Dokumen? (Boleh Dilewati)" (PLAN §7).
- No copy sends readers to another reporting site or platform (`app/messages.test.ts` fails on such names, government reporting domains or "melapor ke Ombudsman"); the 112 emergency line on the report form is a phone number and stays.
- One admin role, never run by the campus: no message says "moderator", and none implies the university, campus officials or a campus service run or endorse the site (`app/messages.test.ts` "one admin role, never run by the campus"). - "Subtuntutan" is one word, like "subkategori" (EYD V: bound forms are written joined); code identifiers and the `#sub-tuntutan` anchor keep their hyphen.

## Motion

The mockup's motion is ported in full (user request), CSS first, with no animation library. Values match the mockup: its curve `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` is practically Linear's `--ease-out-quint` (`cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1)`) and close to the ease-out Stripe uses most (`cubic-bezier(.25, 1, .5, 1)`). The duration tokens that drop to `0s` under reduced motion follow Apple's iPhone page, which sets its enter and exit duration tokens to `0ms` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; Stripe and Linear also switch motion off there. Exits shorter than entries follow NN/g, and CSS before JS follows Vercel's interface guidelines (Sources).

Tokens in `app/styles.css`:

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ease-out` (Tailwind `ease-out`) | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The one signature curve: reveals, stagger, dialog and toast entries, hovers |
| `--ease-scroll` | `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)` | Scroll-linked hero only |
| `--ease-exit` | `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 1, 1)` | Exits |
| `--dur-hover` | 150ms | Buttons, cards, chips, dropzone, fields; also Tailwind's default transition duration, so a bare `transition-colors` uses it |
| `--dur-underline` | 200ms | Nav link underline |
| `--dur-backdrop` | 200ms | Dialog backdrop fade-in |
| `--dur-dialog-in` | 220ms | Dialog entry (opacity, scale .96, translate .5rem) |
| `--dur-toast-in` | 250ms | Toast entry (opacity, translate .75rem); toast exit is instant |
| `--dur-exit` | 150ms | Dialog and backdrop exit; shorter than every entry |
| `--dur-progress` | 200ms | Upload progress bar (linear) |
| `--dur-stagger` and `--step-stagger` | 520ms and 85ms | `.stagger` children; delay is `min(--i, 5) × step` |
| `--dur-reveal` | 700ms | `.reveal` headings (opacity, translate 1.75rem) |
| `--dur-bar` | 640ms | `.bar-grow`: apex table bars grow from the left (`scale: 0 1`, `backwards`), delay `min(--i, 8) × --step-stagger`; CSS only, because the apex ships no JS |

Classes that reuse these tokens: `.confirm-in` (the `dialog-enter` keyframe over `--dur-dialog-in`) for the follow check and the unfollow done icon; `.view-rise` (section headings, opacity and 1.75rem rise) and `.view-rise-list` (children of card and number lists, opacity, 1rem rise and scale .94, staggered by `--i` up to 5) on the apex page, driven by `animation-timeline: view()` with no duration token.

Rules:

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets every `--dur-*` token to `0s`, and the base layer still cuts any other animation or transition to 0.01ms. `app/styles.test.ts` fails if a token is missing from the reduce block or an exit is not shorter than the entries.
- Content is visible by default. `.reveal` and `.stagger` hide content, so only JS adds them, and only to elements still offscreen at mount; no markup ships them (`app/styles.test.ts`).
- Entrances and exits move only transform and opacity. Hovers may fade colours. Nothing animates layout properties; reserve the space of anything that moves, so motion never shifts layout.
- Home hero (KM ITB tenant only): the mockup scroll choreography as CSS scroll-driven animation (`.hero-scroll`, unlayered in `app/styles.css`, 0 KB JS). The text column fades (opacity and translate only, so the H1 and lead stay in the accessibility tree); only the `.hero-actions` wrapper (search card and report card) ends `visibility: hidden`, so invisible controls take no focus or click. While scrolled past the hero, Tab skips those controls; Home or PageUp brings them back. The graphic has no `will-change`, so Chrome rasterises it at the largest keyframe scale and the 1.45 bubble stays sharp. The bubble moves to the centre, grows to 1.45 and drops out while the light background fades to the band. Cara Kerja rises under the stage (`.hero-scroll` ends with `margin-bottom: -70vh`); its section is `relative`, so it paints above the sticky stage, and the graphic fades out between 52% and 60% of the hero timeline (`hero-fade-late`), before it reaches the Cara Kerja heading (`app/styles.test.ts`, `app/features/home/home.test.tsx`). Active only at `min-width: 64rem`, `min-height: 54rem`, `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`; the height gate keeps the whole text column, report card included, on screen while the stage is sticky. Everything else gets the static hero.
- Reveal and stagger where the code uses them today: Home (section headings, Cara Kerja list, the "Tuntutan yang Dikawal" register, showcase lists), `/data` (section headings, the Angka Utama numbers, the "Tuntutan Publik" register and the Peringkat Isu rows; the two tuntutan registers use `useStagger` on the `<ol>` with `--i` per row), and the staff lists `/kelola/anggota` (member list) and `/admin` (campus list), which use `useStagger` only. The apex national home ships no JS, so it uses the CSS-only `.view-rise` and `.view-rise-list` and `.bar-grow`, declared only under `@media screen and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())` (`app/styles.test.ts` checks the guard), so content is visible in browsers without the feature, under reduced motion and in print. Other working pages (`/isu`, `/tuntutan`, `/tuntutan/:code`, `/lapor/*`, `/lacak`, `/masuk`, the rest of `/kelola` including the tuntutan pages) get hover, press, dialog and toast motion only. With emulated `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `/` and `/data` carry no `.stagger` or `.reveal` class (browser check). The stagger on the two staff lists departs from the earlier "Home only" rule and is recorded here as built; remove it there if the rule should stay strict.
- No motion on keyboard-repeated actions: focus rings, the mobile menu and copy-button state change instantly.
- Never: large-area parallax, autoplay video, animated gradients, `backdrop-filter` over scrolling content, shake on error, page transitions.

- Categories (all pairs pass): programme `#176bff`, disaster `#d81623`, social aid `#c8279a` (`CATEGORY_COLOR`, fixed in every tenant; `chart-theme.test.ts` checks 3:1 on the white card). Purple `#a500fd` failed against blue for deuteranopia (ΔE 0.5), so social aid uses magenta in charts.
- Two-series trend: `#176bff` and `#e57b12` pass CVD, but orange is under 3:1, so every series has a direct label and a data table.
- Ordinal ramp (status flow): `#86b6ef`, `#5598e7`, `#2a78d6`, `#1c5cab`, `#104281`.
- Good and critical status: `#0ca30c` and `#d03b3b`, always with an icon and a word.
- Chart ink: primary `#151515`, secondary `#5e5e5e`, muted `#7c7c7c`, grid `#ece6d6`, axis `#d9cfb6`.
- Every chart has a data table; the map has a region table (PLAN §9).
- Map classes, fixed per measure and never per view (`shared/dashboard.ts`, `mapClass(count, classes)`, PLAN §5.9). Laporan layer (`MAP_CLASSES`): 0 `#dde2ea`, 1–4 `#86b6ef`, 5–19 `#5598e7`, 20–49 `#2a78d6`, 50 or more `#104281` (the ordinal ramp; dataviz `validate_palette.js --ordinal --mode light --surface #ffffff` on the four steps: monotone, adjacent ΔL at least 0.06, light end 2.11:1, hue spread 3°). Tuntutan layer (`DEMAND_MAP_CLASSES`, `DEMAND_FILL` in `chart-theme.ts`, fixed hex, never the tenant token): 0 `#dde2ea`, 1 `#ad97e8`, 2 `#8a6bd8`, 3–4 `#6b3fb8` (the `pop-strong` default), 5 or more `#3f2180`, built toward blue-violet away from the magenta Bansos colour (validator: all pass, light end 2.51:1, hue spread 5°). With zero in front both ramps stay monotone; zero is 1.30:1 on white; the lightest steps against zero are violet 1.93:1 and blue 1.62:1. `chart-theme.test.ts` checks that both ramps darken step by step and the light end is 2:1 or more. A hatched "Tidak ada data" appears only on the Laporan layer when a province has no entry. Province borders are white; the legend always lists every class of its layer. Two layers, two hues, so "dark" never means the same number on both.
- Small n (Starting Values, `MIN_PUBLIC_N`): private report counts of 1 to 4 are stored as null by the worker and read "1–4", never 0; a share needs a denominator of at least 5. The Laporan map cells count verified reports, so 1–4 there is disclosure control (`publicCount`); the reasons table hides 1–4 too, and so does the apex national view (`national_aggregates`). Tenant totals (laporan masuk, laporan terverifikasi, isu terbit) stay exact, and so does the monthly report trend: an exact total refreshed every 15 minutes lets anyone recover a hidden month, or a single hidden province, by subtraction, so the trend draws every month and "Cara mengolah data ini" says report counts are exact. Issue counts in the charts (category, stage, resolution, response metrics) are exact, because every counted issue is public on `/isu`; tuntutan counts are exact public admin content. Numeric table cells never wrap, so "1–4" stays whole.
- Fonts: chart text and table cells `font-ui` (Barlow on KM ITB); numbers bold body font, as the mockup.

### Map Boundaries

`app/features/data/provinces.topo.json` holds the 38 provinces, keyed by Kemendagri code with cahyadsn/wilayah names (MIT). Geometry: OpenStreetMap `admin_level=4` relations (all 38 carry `ref:ID:kemendagri`), polygons from polygons.openstreetmap.fr, clipped to the OSM simplified land polygons (osmdata.openstreetmap.de, 13 Oct 2026) because the province relations include sea areas, islands under 100 km² dropped, simplified to 2% (mapshaper 0.7.70, run once from a scratch script that is not in the repo), quantised to 20,000. The topology is read only on the server. The projected paths in `provinces.svg` are public (the map loads them), so that file carries an ODbL notice and is released under ODbL as PLAN §5.9 requires for downloadable derived data; the page carries PLAN §5.9's attribution line. No BIG data is used. Licence and attribution terms: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/; land polygons: https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/data/land-polygons.html. Replace it with the PostGIS import from the Phase 0 spike when that lands.

## Email

Emails (`server/emails/layout.ts`, `render.ts`) carry the same voice as the site in a layout mail clients render: system fonts, ink `#08266e`, soft ink `#2c3a66`, surface `#fff8e5` behind a raised `#ffffff` 600 px column, line `#d5d9e4`, and the tenant `theme.brand` (default `#0f55d6`) for the top bar and the one square action button. Facts sit in a table with left `th` labels and vertically centred cells; long URLs hide behind link labels; every follower email ends with "Anda menerima email ini karena mengikuti isu {code}." (or its tuntutan twin, with the tuntutan code) and the per-issue or per-tuntutan unfollow link. A tuntutan mail carries the code, title, "{n} dari {total} subtuntutan dipenuhi" and the link. Complaint mails name "isu X" or "tuntutan X". The sign-in code mail has the heading "Kode Verifikasi Suara Madani" (`email_otp_heading`) and the subject "Kode verifikasi Suara Madani"; the invitation mail is "Undangan menjadi admin {operator} di Suara Madani" with the facts Organisasi and Peran (Admin). The WhatsApp code is one short plain-text line with no link (lower ban risk). Every email has an HTML and a text part.

## Forbidden ("Too AI")

- Rounded white cards with shadows, rows of identical boxes for content that is really a list (the `/tentang` mockup cards above are the one exception: square, with the mockup's soft shadow in the KM ITB set only).
- Pill buttons (`rounded-full` is for real circles only) and red to pink to purple gradients.
- Gradient text, blurred blobs in corners.
- Small uppercase tracked labels above headings (eyebrows), arrows at the end of button text.
- A long one-sentence title in a section heading: move the sentence into `description`.
- Dark 3D, glass spheres, black neon backgrounds.

Quick check: `rg -nP "rounded-(2xl|3xl|full)|shadow-(?!floating|toast|about-)|uppercase|tracking-" app/components app/features app/routes` should only find real circles, the letter-spaced report and verification codes, and the About display headings and operator strip (`features/content/display-title.tsx`, `routes/pages/about.tsx`), whose `uppercase` is the mockup's display style over Title Case text, not an eyebrow.
